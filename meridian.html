Privacy Policy — Meridian World Time

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy applies to Meridian World Time for Wear OS, package com.aiinventions.meridian.

Watch-face data

Meridian World Time is a resource-only watch face rendered by Wear OS. The watch face contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, account system, server connection or executable application code. The developer does not receive data from the watch face.

Time, the selected time zone, the date and display preferences are used locally to draw the dial. Preferences are stored and managed by Wear OS. The map's approximate day/night display is calculated from time and date, without GPS or location permission. No location is sent to the developer.

Complications and other services

You may choose complication providers to display weather, battery level, date, activity or other information. Wear OS supplies the selected provider's display data to the watch face for local rendering. Meridian does not transmit that data to the developer or to another service.

Providers are separate apps or system services. They may request permissions, use sensors, process location or access the internet under their own privacy policies. Their behavior is not controlled by Meridian World Time. Google Play, Wear OS and your device manufacturer may process purchases, diagnostics, backups or account information under their own policies. The watch face itself does not process payments or receive payment-card details.

Retention and deletion

The developer maintains no server-side watch-face data to retain or delete. You can change or remove complications in the face editor and remove the watch face using your device's controls. Device backups and provider data are governed by the relevant platform or provider. Meridian does not create a user account.

Support and privacy contact

Public support email: meridian@biteballot.app

If you choose to contact support, your message and email address are used to respond to your request. Do not send sensitive complication or health information unless you want to include it in the conversation. You may request deletion of support correspondence using the same contact address, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Changes

This page will be updated if the app's data practices change. The date above identifies this version of the policy.
